    About The Vishva Sabha

    The Vishva Sabha, meaning World Assembly, is an organization committed to bringing Model United Nations experiences to schools and colleges. We work with educational institutions to plan and conduct structured, engaging, and professionally managed MUN conferences. Through debate, diplomacy, negotiation, and collaboration, we aim to help students become more confident, informed, and responsible leaders.

    Our Mission

    To make Model United Nations accessible to students by helping schools and colleges organize engaging, educational, and professionally managed conferences.

    Our Vision

    To build a generation of confident, articulate, diplomatic, and globally aware young leaders who can think critically, communicate effectively, and contribute positively to society.

    Our Core Values

    The Vishva Sabha is an independent organization and is not affiliated with the United Nations or any government.
